06 Net Metering & Government Subsidy Process
Subsidy credit flow and bidirectional net meter integration
PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

The Central Government provides financial assistance (CFA) under the PM Surya Ghar national portal. As an MNRE-empanelled EPC vendor, Enermass facilitates complete document submission and structural validation. Once the net meter is commissioned and inspected by DISCOM authorities, the subsidy amount is credited directly to the customer's linked bank account.

Subsidy Details for Your Quote
PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Amount: ₹78,000
Note: Subsidy calculations are based on official government guidelines: ₹30,000 per kW for first 2 kW, and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW.
Net Metering Integration & Export

Net metering allows you to export excess solar energy generated during daytime hours back to the grid. Your DISCOM bi-directional meter records both import (grid consumption) and export (solar excess). You will receive credits on your monthly billing invoice for all exported units, substantially dropping your net cost.

Step-by-Step Timeline
  • 1. Site survey & feasibility
  • 2. DISCOM Feasibility approval
  • 3. Plant installation (EPC)
  • 4. Work completion report
  • 5. Joint inspection & Net Metering
  • 6. Subsidy release (60-90 days)
Liaison Support
Enermass manages all application filings on the PM Surya Ghar and DISCOM portals, handling structural clearances and liaison with the electrical inspectorate.
